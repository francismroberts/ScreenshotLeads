1 Click Social Media Leads

Ever spot someone on social media you want to reach out to, but turning their profile into a lead feels like too much work?

The Creator Lead Generator Shortcut captures leads in one click. Go from their profile to a Google Sheet as you browse.

Works on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, or X on iPhone and Mac.

  1. Open a profile Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, or X
  2. Trigger the shortcut Action Button, Back Tap, or a keyboard shortcut
  3. AI reads the screen It picks out the creator’s details
  4. Lead added A new row in your lead tracker

You’ll need

  • A supported device iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or a Mac with Apple silicon, on the latest software
  • Apple Intelligence on In Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri.Also turn on the ChatGPT extension there for the ChatGPT version.
  • A Google account For your own form and lead tracker

Messages you’ll see

  • Saved: Creator Name & Platform

    It worked. The creator is in your lead tracker.

  • Couldn’t read a profile here. Open the creator’s profile page and try again.

    Nothing was saved. Make sure you’re on the creator’s actual profile page, not a video, post, or About page.

  • Not saved. Your form link may be wrong, or the form isn’t published.

    Check that your form is published (Step 1). If it is, delete the shortcut and redo Step 2 with your form’s full URL.

Troubleshooting

  • Saved, but some details are wrong

    Use the main profile page, with the follower count showing.

  • Can’t make a copy of the form

    Sign in to Google, then open the link again.

  • No Apple Intelligence in Settings

    Check that your device is supported (see You’ll need) and that your device language and Siri language match.

Good to know

  • It reads only what’s on screen

    AI can misread, and long bio links can get cut off. Double-check key details, and open the Profile Link for the full bio.

  • Your data, your sheet

    The screenshot goes to the AI you picked. The details go to your own form.

  • What each lead includes

    Name, Handle, Profile Link, Platform, Followers, and Category, plus Location, Snapchat, Email, and Bio Links when they’re shown on the profile. Each row also gets a Status (starts as “New”) and a blank Notes column for tracking your outreach.
